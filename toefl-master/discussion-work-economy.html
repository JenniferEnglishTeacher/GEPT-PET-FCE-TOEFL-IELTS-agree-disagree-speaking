Discussion 06 — Will artificial intelligence replace a large number of human workers? 人工智慧會取代大量的人類工作者嗎？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are discussing the future of work. Artificial intelligence can now write reports, analyze data and answer customers' questions, and many companies are already using it to cut costs. Some experts predict that AI will replace a large number of human workers within the next twenty years. Others believe that AI will mainly change jobs rather than destroy them, and that it will even create new ones. In your opinion, will AI replace a large number of human workers? Why or why not?

David: I think AI will replace a large number of human workers. AI systems can process huge amounts of data, work twenty-four hours a day and make fewer mistakes, and they cost far less than employees. Companies always want to reduce their costs, so they have a strong reason to automate as many jobs as possible.

Mary: I disagree. AI lacks creativity, empathy and moral judgment, so it cannot do jobs that depend on human relationships, such as teaching, nursing or counseling. In my view, AI is a tool that makes workers more productive rather than a replacement for them. History also shows that new technology creates new kinds of jobs.

Discussion 14 — Should companies allow employees to work from home most of the time? 公司應該讓員工大部分時間在家工作嗎？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are discussing how the workplace is changing. Thanks to video calls and cloud software, many office jobs can now be done from home, and some companies let their employees work remotely most of the week. Supporters say that remote work saves time and makes people more productive, while critics worry that it weakens teamwork and makes it harder to separate work from private life. In your opinion, should companies allow employees to work from home most of the time? Why or why not?

David: I think companies should let employees work from home most of the time. Without a long commute, workers gain one or two extra hours every day, and they can concentrate better at home because there are fewer interruptions from colleagues and meetings. People who have more control over their schedule are also happier, so they work harder.

Mary: I disagree. Good ideas often come from casual conversations in the office, and those are hard to have through a screen. Besides, many people cannot focus at home because of family members, housework or their phones. When your home becomes your office, it is also difficult to stop working, which leads to stress. I think employees should work in the office most of the time.

Essay A — Agree with David（支持在家工作 · about 150 words）

1. Position 表明立場
In my opinion 依我之見 / allow … to + V 允許…做 / work from home 在家工作 / I agree with David's point that 我同意 David 的論點 / remote work 遠距工作 / save time 節省時間		 In my opinion, companies should allow employees to work from home most of the time, and I agree with David's point that remote work saves time.
2. New reason 補充新理由
What David did not mention is that David 沒有提到的是 / widen 擴大 / talent pool 人才庫 / location 地點 / no longer matter 不再重要 / hire 僱用 / position 職位 / This benefits not only A but also B 這不只有利於 A，也有利於 B / employer 雇主 / the economy as a whole 整體經濟		 What David did not mention is that remote work also widens the talent pool. When location no longer matters, companies can hire the best person for a position, including parents of young children and people who live far from big cities. This benefits not only employers but also the economy as a whole.
3. Example 舉例
… is a good example …是個好例子 / take care of 照顧 / yet 然而 / work full-time 全職工作 / software engineer 軟體工程師		 My aunt is a good example. She lives in Hualien and takes care of my grandmother, yet she works full-time as a software engineer for a company in Taipei.
4. Respond to Mary 回應對方
… argues that …主張 / casual conversation 閒聊 / While this may be true 雖然這也許是事實 / meet in person 親自見面 / creativity 創造力 / do not have to suffer 不一定會受影響		 Mary argues that good ideas come from casual conversations in the office. While this may be true, teams can still meet in person once a week, so creativity does not have to suffer.
5. Conclusion 結論
For these reasons, I am convinced that 基於這些理由，我深信 / flexible working arrangements 彈性的工作安排 / benefit both A and B 對 A 和 B 都有利 / employee 員工 / employer 雇主		 For these reasons, I am convinced that flexible working arrangements benefit both employees and employers.
