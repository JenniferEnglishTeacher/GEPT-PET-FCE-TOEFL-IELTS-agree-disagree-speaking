Email 06 — Giving Advice to a Stressed Cousin 給考試壓力大的表妹建議

Recipient 收件人：Emma, your cousin 表妹艾瑪（informal 非正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
Your cousin Emma, who is in her final year of high school, has sent you a message saying that she feels very stressed about her upcoming final exams. She studies until late every night, but she cannot concentrate and is afraid that she will fail. You took the same exams two years ago, so you understand how she feels, and you would like to share the methods that worked for you. Write an email to Emma. In your email, do the following:
  • Show that you understand how she feels
  • Give her two pieces of advice
  • Offer to help her
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Hi + first name 名字（informal 非正式）		 Hi Emma,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I hope you are doing well 希望你一切都好 / I am writing to 我寫信是想 / give … some advice 給…一些建議		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to give you some advice about your exams.
Bullet 1 — Show understanding 表示理解
I am sorry to hear that 很遺憾聽到 / stressed 有壓力的 / It is completely normal to + V …是完全正常的 / nervous 緊張的		 I am sorry to hear that you have been feeling stressed. It is completely normal to feel nervous, especially when the exams are so important.
Bullet 2 — Two pieces of advice 兩個建議
First, I suggest + V-ing 首先，我建議 / study schedule 讀書計畫表 / so that 以便 / manage your time 管理時間 / Second, remember to + V 其次，記得要 / take short breaks 短暫休息 / concentrate 專心		 First, I suggest making a simple study schedule so that you can manage your time better. Second, remember to take short breaks and get enough sleep, because resting helps you concentrate.
Bullet 3 — Offer help 主動幫忙
If you would like, 如果你願意的話 / study together online 線上一起讀書 / I can also help you + V 我也可以幫你 / review 複習 / difficult subjects 困難的科目		 If you would like, we can study together online this weekend. I can also help you review the difficult subjects.
Closing 結尾
Please do not worry too much 請不要太擔心 / I am sure that 我確定 / do well 表現好		 Please do not worry too much. I am sure that you will do well.
Sign-off 署名
Take care, 保重		 Take care,
Jennifer

Sample 2 — Level 2 進階版（Score 5 · CEFR B2–C1 · about 135 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + first name 名字（friendly but polite 親切而有禮）		 Dear Emma,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing in response to 我寫信是為了回覆 / upcoming 即將到來的 / examinations 考試		 I am writing in response to your message about your upcoming examinations.
Bullet 1 — Show understanding 表示理解
I was sorry to learn that 得知…我很難過 / overwhelmed 不堪負荷的 / Such pressure is entirely understandable 這樣的壓力完全可以理解 / particularly when 尤其當 / expectations 期望		 I was sorry to learn that you have been feeling overwhelmed. Such pressure is entirely understandable, particularly when expectations are high, and I felt exactly the same two years ago.
Bullet 2 — Two pieces of advice 兩個建議
One effective strategy would be to + V 一個有效的策略是 / establish 建立 / revision timetable 複習時間表 / as this can 因為這能 / anxiety 焦慮 / manageable tasks 可處理的小任務 / In addition, I would strongly recommend + V-ing 此外，我強烈建議 / adequate rest 充足的休息 / which can significantly improve 這能大幅提升 / concentration 專注力		 One effective strategy would be to establish a structured revision timetable, as this can turn anxiety into manageable tasks. In addition, I would strongly recommend getting adequate rest and taking short breaks between study sessions, which can significantly improve both concentration and memory.
Bullet 3 — Offer help 主動幫忙
If it would ease your concerns 如果這能減輕你的擔憂 / I would be more than happy to + V 我非常樂意 / challenging topics 較難的主題 / practice papers 模擬試卷		 If it would ease your concerns, I would be more than happy to review challenging topics with you or go through practice papers together online this weekend.
Closing 結尾
Please remember that 請記得 / face … alone 獨自面對 / I am confident in your ability to + V 我對你…的能力有信心 / succeed 成功		 Please remember that you are not facing this alone, and I am confident in your ability to succeed.
Sign-off 署名
Warm regards, 溫馨的問候		 Warm regards,
Jennifer
