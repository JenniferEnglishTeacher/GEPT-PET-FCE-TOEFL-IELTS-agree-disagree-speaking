Email 08 — Thanking a Teaching Assistant for Extra Help 感謝助教課後輔導

Recipient 收件人：Mr. Brown, the teaching assistant 助教布朗先生（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a first-year university student taking an introductory chemistry course. Two weeks ago, you were struggling to understand chemical bonding, so Mr. Brown, the teaching assistant, stayed after class twice to explain the topic and gave you extra practice questions. Last week you took the midterm exam and did much better than you had expected. You would like to thank him and keep improving. Write an email to Mr. Brown, the teaching assistant. In your email, do the following:
  • Thank him for the specific help he gave you
  • Tell him about the result of your midterm exam
  • Ask a follow-up question about preparing for the next unit
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + Mr. / Ms. + last name 姓氏（formal 正式）		 Dear Mr. Brown,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to thank you for 我寫信是想感謝您 / help me with 幫助我… / midterm exam 期中考		 I am writing to thank you for helping me with chemistry before the midterm exam.
Bullet 1 — Thank him for the specific help 感謝具體的幫助
Thank you so much for + V-ing 非常感謝您… / stay after class 課後留下來 / explain 解釋 / chemical bonding 化學鍵 / practice questions 練習題 / help me understand … much better 幫助我更了解		 Thank you so much for staying after class to explain chemical bonding. Your clear examples and extra practice questions helped me understand the topic much better.
Bullet 2 — The result of the exam 考試結果
I am happy to tell you that 我很高興告訴您 / score 分數 / semester 學期 / I could not have done it without your help 沒有您的幫忙我做不到		 I am happy to tell you that I got 85 on the exam. It was my best score this semester, and I could not have done it without your help.
Bullet 3 — Ask a follow-up question 提出後續問題
I would also like to ask 我也想請問 / Could you recommend 您可以推薦…嗎 / materials 教材 / the next unit 下一個單元		 I would also like to ask you a question. Could you recommend some practice materials for the next unit?
Closing 結尾
Thank you again for 再次感謝您的 / I look forward to hearing from you 期待您的回覆		 Thank you again for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, 誠摯的問候		 Best regards,
Jennifer

Email 16 — Following Up on a Group Project 追蹤小組報告進度

Recipient 收件人：Kevin, your project partner 小組報告夥伴 Kevin（semi-formal 半正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You and your classmate Kevin are working together on a group presentation about renewable energy for your Environmental Science class. The presentation is due next Monday. You have already finished your research section, but the presentation slides are not complete and the speaking parts have not been divided yet. You have sent Kevin several messages this week, but he has not replied, and you are worried that you will not finish on time. Write an email to Kevin, your project partner. In your email, do the following:
  • Remind him about the project and its deadline
  • Explain what still needs to be done
  • Suggest a time to talk about the project
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Hi + first name 名字（classmate 同學 · semi-formal 半正式）		 Hi Kevin,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I hope you are doing well 希望你一切都好 / I am writing to follow up on 我寫信是想追蹤…的進度 / group project 小組報告		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to follow up on our group project.
Bullet 1 — Remind him about the project and its deadline 提醒報告與截止日
I just wanted to remind you that 我只是想提醒你 / presentation 簡報 / renewable energy 再生能源 / be due + 時間 …截止 / I have not heard from you yet 我還沒收到你的消息		 I just wanted to remind you that our presentation on renewable energy is due next Monday. I sent you a few messages this week, but I have not heard from you yet.
Bullet 2 — Explain what still needs to be done 說明還沒完成的部分
I have already finished 我已經完成 / research section 研究部分 / However, we still need to + V 然而我們還需要 / slides 投影片 / divide the speaking parts 分配口說段落		 I have already finished my research section. However, we still need to complete the slides and divide the speaking parts.
Bullet 3 — Suggest a time to talk 提議討論時間
Would you be available to + V 你有空…嗎 / talk online 線上討論 / If that time does not work for you 如果那個時間你不方便 / suggest another time 提議另一個時間		 Would you be available to talk online tomorrow evening at 7 p.m.? If that time does not work for you, please suggest another time.
Closing 結尾
I hope we can + V 我希望我們能 / on time 準時 / Please let me know soon 請盡快讓我知道		 I hope we can finish everything on time. Please let me know soon.
Sign-off 署名
Best, 祝好		 Best,
Jennifer
