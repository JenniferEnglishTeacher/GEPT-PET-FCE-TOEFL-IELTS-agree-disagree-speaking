Discussion 02 — Will AI-generated images and videos have a positive or a negative influence on society? AI 生成的圖片與影片對社會的影響是正面還是負面？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are looking at generative AI. Today, anyone can create realistic images and videos in seconds simply by typing a short description. Some people welcome this technology because it makes creative work faster and cheaper, while others worry that it will fill the internet with fake content and threaten the jobs of human creators. Do you think AI-generated images and videos will have a positive or a negative influence on society? Why?

David: I believe AI-generated images and videos will have a positive influence. They allow creators to produce high-quality content quickly and at a lower cost. For example, teachers can use AI visuals to explain complex ideas more clearly, and filmmakers can create realistic scenes without expensive equipment. This makes creativity accessible to everyone.

Mary: I disagree with David. AI-generated content can easily be misused to spread misinformation, manipulate public opinion, or create fake videos that look real, which makes it harder to trust what we see online. Moreover, if AI replaces human creators, it may reduce job opportunities and weaken the value of original work.

Discussion 10 — Should AI be used to assess students' academic work and employees' performance? 應該用 AI 來評量學生的課業與員工的工作表現嗎？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are discussing artificial intelligence and evaluation. More and more universities use AI tools to grade essays and assignments, and many companies use AI systems to measure how well their employees perform. Supporters say these systems are fast and objective, while critics argue that a machine cannot truly understand human work. Should AI be used to assess students' academic work and employees' performance? Why or why not?

David: I believe AI should be used in assessment because it increases efficiency and reduces human bias. AI grading tools can evaluate assignments and performance data quickly and consistently. Human evaluators may be influenced by personal preferences or tiredness, whereas AI applies the same standard to everyone.

Mary: I disagree with David. Although AI can process data efficiently, it cannot fully understand creativity, context or emotion. A student's original ideas may not fit standard patterns, and performance at work involves teamwork and leadership, which AI may misjudge. Human judgment remains essential for fair evaluation.

Essay A — Agree with David（支持 AI 評量 · about 150 words）

1. Position 表明立場
agree with David 同意 David / assess 評量 / evaluation 評估 / both faster and fairer 既更快又更公平		 I agree with David that AI should be used to assess students and employees, because it makes evaluation both faster and fairer.
2. New reason 補充新理由
I would add that 我想補充 / immediate feedback 即時回饋 / comments 評語 / by then 到那時 / weaknesses 弱點 / right away 立刻		 I would add that AI also provides immediate feedback. People often wait days or weeks for a teacher's or manager's comments, and by then they have forgotten the details of their work. With AI, they can see their weaknesses and correct them right away.
3. Example 舉例
For example 舉例來說 / AI assessment tool AI 評量工具 / point out 指出 / within seconds 幾秒內 / revise 修改 / corrections 批改		 For example, when I practise English writing with an AI assessment tool, it points out my grammar mistakes within seconds. I can revise my essay immediately, so I have improved much faster than when I waited a week for my teacher's corrections.
4. Respond to Mary 回應對方

5. Conclusion 結論
For these reasons 基於這些理由 / a regular part of …的常態 / as long as 只要 / remain in charge 仍然掌握主導權		 For these reasons, AI should become a regular part of assessment as long as humans remain in charge.
