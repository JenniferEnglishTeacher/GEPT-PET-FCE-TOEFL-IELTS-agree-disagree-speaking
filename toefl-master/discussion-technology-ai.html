Discussion 02 — Will AI-generated images and videos have a positive or a negative influence on society? AI 生成的圖片與影片對社會的影響是正面還是負面？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are looking at generative AI. Today, anyone can create realistic images and videos in seconds simply by typing a short description. Some people welcome this technology because it makes creative work faster and cheaper, while others worry that it will fill the internet with fake content and threaten the jobs of human creators. Do you think AI-generated images and videos will have a positive or a negative influence on society? Why?

David: I believe AI-generated images and videos will have a positive influence. They allow creators to produce high-quality content quickly and at a lower cost. For example, teachers can use AI visuals to explain complex ideas more clearly, and filmmakers can create realistic scenes without expensive equipment. This makes creativity accessible to everyone.

Mary: I disagree with David. AI-generated content can easily be misused to spread misinformation, manipulate public opinion, or create fake videos that look real, which makes it harder to trust what we see online. Moreover, if AI replaces human creators, it may reduce job opportunities and weaken the value of original work.

Discussion 10 — Should AI be used to assess students' academic work and employees' performance? 應該用 AI 來評量學生的課業與員工的工作表現嗎？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are discussing artificial intelligence and evaluation. More and more universities use AI tools to grade essays and assignments, and many companies use AI systems to measure how well their employees perform. Supporters say these systems are fast and objective, while critics argue that a machine cannot truly understand human work. Should AI be used to assess students' academic work and employees' performance? Why or why not?

David: I believe AI should be used in assessment because it increases efficiency and reduces human bias. AI grading tools can evaluate assignments and performance data quickly and consistently. Human evaluators may be influenced by personal preferences or tiredness, whereas AI applies the same standard to everyone.

Mary: I disagree with David. Although AI can process data efficiently, it cannot fully understand creativity, context or emotion. A student's original ideas may not fit standard patterns, and performance at work involves teamwork and leadership, which AI may misjudge. Human judgment remains essential for fair evaluation.

Discussion 18 — Should universities encourage students to use AI tools such as ChatGPT in their studies? 大學應該鼓勵學生在課業上使用 ChatGPT 等 AI 工具嗎？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are looking at how artificial intelligence is changing the way students learn. AI tools such as ChatGPT can summarize research papers, explain difficult ideas and answer questions at any time of day, and many university students already use them every week. Some educators welcome these tools, while others worry about their effect on students' skills. In your opinion, should universities encourage students to use AI tools such as ChatGPT in their studies? Why or why not?

David: I strongly feel that AI tools are a real benefit for higher education, so students should be encouraged to use them. ChatGPT can analyze and summarize long research papers, which makes complex topics easier to understand. It is also like having a tutor who is available 24 hours a day, which is especially helpful when students are preparing for exams.

Mary: I see the benefits David mentioned, but I am worried about the downsides. If students rely too much on AI to write essays or solve problems, they may never develop essential skills such as critical thinking and independent problem-solving. There is also a risk of misinformation, because AI's answers are not always accurate. Universities should not encourage students to depend on it.

Discussion 26 — Overall, is AI a dangerous technology that will harm society, or a powerful tool that will improve people's lives? 整體而言，AI 是會危害社會的危險科技，還是能改善人們生活的強大工具？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are discussing the overall impact of artificial intelligence. AI can now write reports, help doctors diagnose diseases, drive cars and answer almost any question within seconds. Some experts see it as one of the most useful inventions in history, while others warn that it is developing too quickly and could do serious damage. I want you to consider the following question: overall, is AI a dangerous technology that will harm society, or a powerful tool that will improve people's lives? Why?

David: I'm of the opinion that AI is a powerful tool that will improve our lives. It has the potential to help solve many of the world's biggest problems, from climate change to disease. In addition, AI can help us make better decisions by analyzing vast amounts of data and identifying patterns that humans may miss. Of course, we must make sure that it is developed in a responsible way.

Mary: I disagree with David. Personally, I think AI is a threat. As it advances, many jobs will become automated, leaving people without work and increasing income inequality. There is also a risk that AI systems could be programmed with biases and discriminate against certain groups. Since we cannot guarantee that AI is being developed in an ethical way right now, I think it is best to slow down.
