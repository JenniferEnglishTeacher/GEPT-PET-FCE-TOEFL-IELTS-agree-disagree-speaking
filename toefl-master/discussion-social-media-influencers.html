Discussion 03 — Do social media influencers have a mainly positive or a mainly negative influence on young people? 社群媒體網紅對年輕人的影響主要是正面還是負面？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are discussing digital media and society. Social media influencers now reach millions of young followers every day, and many teenagers seem to trust them more than traditional celebrities or advertisements. Some people see influencers as inspiring role models who share useful knowledge, while others believe they encourage materialism and unrealistic lifestyles. In your opinion, do social media influencers have a mainly positive or a mainly negative influence on young people? Why?

David: I think influencers have a mainly positive influence. Many of them share free, practical knowledge about topics such as fitness, cooking, study skills and personal finance in a way that is easy to understand. They also show young people that it is possible to turn a passion into a career through creativity and hard work.

Mary: I see it differently. Many influencers are paid to promote products, yet their recommendations sound like friendly advice, so young followers are easily persuaded to buy things they do not need. In addition, the perfect bodies and luxurious lifestyles they display are often edited or staged, which can damage teenagers' self-esteem.

Discussion 11 — Should the live streaming of dangerous stunts be banned? 應該禁止直播危險的特技與挑戰嗎？

Time 時間：10 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：at least 100 words（samples: about 150）

Professor (Dr. Miller): This week we are looking at how online platforms should deal with risky content. Live streaming allows creators to broadcast to millions of viewers in real time, and some streamers attempt dangerous stunts, such as climbing skyscrapers without ropes or taking part in extreme challenges, in order to attract attention. Supporters of a ban say that such broadcasts put both streamers and viewers in danger, while opponents argue that a ban would be ineffective and unfair to responsible creators. In your opinion, should the live streaming of dangerous stunts be banned? Why or why not?

David: I think live streaming dangerous stunts should be banned because it puts lives at serious risk. When people broadcast dangerous challenges in real time, viewers, especially teenagers, may imitate them without fully understanding the consequences. Some streamers even take greater risks to gain attention, likes or donations, so dangerous behaviour is rewarded rather than discouraged.

Mary: I understand the risks, but I don't think a ban is the right solution. Live streaming itself is just a tool, and responsibility should lie with the individual. Instead of banning this content, platforms should strengthen warnings, age restrictions and content moderation. Education and guidance are more effective than outright bans.
