Email 02 — Requesting a Recommendation Letter 請教授寫推薦信

Recipient 收件人：Professor Wang 王教授（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a third-year university student, and you plan to apply for a one-semester exchange program at a university in Canada. The application must include a recommendation letter from a professor, and the deadline is the 30th of this month. Last year you took Professor Wang's Academic Writing course. You received a high grade and often took part in class discussions, so you believe she knows your work well. Write an email to Professor Wang. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain what you are applying for and why
  • Ask her to write a recommendation letter for you
  • Tell her the deadline and offer the materials she may need
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + title + family name 職稱＋姓氏		 Dear Professor Wang,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
hope … doing well / ask for your help 請您幫忙 / application 申請		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to ask for your help with an application.
Bullet 1 — What and why 申請內容與原因
plan to apply for 打算申請 / exchange program 交換學生計畫 / next semester 下學期 / experience a different culture 體驗不同文化		 I am planning to apply for an exchange program in Canada next semester because I want to improve my English and experience a different culture.
Bullet 2 — Request 請求
I was wondering if you could… 不知您是否能… / recommendation letter 推薦信 / learned a lot 學到很多 / know my work well 了解我的表現		 I was wondering if you could write a recommendation letter for me. I learned a lot in your Academic Writing course last year, so I believe you know my work well.
Bullet 3 — Deadline + materials 截止日與資料
deadline 截止日 / résumé 履歷 / transcript 成績單 / if you need them 若您需要		 The deadline is the 30th of this month. I can send you my résumé and transcript if you need them.
Closing 結尾
thank … for your time 感謝撥冗 / look forward to hearing from you 期待您的回覆		 Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, 謹致問候		 Best regards,
Jennifer

Sample 2 — Level 2 進階版（Score 5 · CEFR B2–C1 · about 135 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + title + family name 職稱＋姓氏		 Dear Professor Wang,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
finds you well 一切安好 / whether you would be willing to 您是否願意 / support my application 支持我的申請		 I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to ask whether you would be willing to support my application for an exchange program.
Bullet 1 — What and why 申請內容與原因
intend to 打算 / one-semester 一學期的 / studying abroad 出國留學 / would allow me to 能讓我 / strengthen 加強 / broaden my horizons 拓展視野		 I intend to apply for a one-semester exchange program at a university in Canada, as studying abroad would allow me to strengthen my academic English and broaden my horizons.
Bullet 2 — Request 請求
I would be grateful if you could… 若您能…我將非常感激 / on my behalf 代表我、為我 / Having + p.p. 分詞構句 / be familiar with 熟悉 / class participation 課堂參與		 I would be grateful if you could write a recommendation letter on my behalf. Having taken your Academic Writing course last year, I believe you are familiar with my written work and class participation.
Bullet 3 — Deadline + materials 截止日與資料
application deadline 申請截止日 / To make the process easier 為了讓流程更順利 / provide 提供 / personal statement 自傳、讀書計畫		 The application deadline is the 30th of this month. To make the process easier, I would be happy to provide my résumé, transcript and personal statement.
Closing 結尾
I completely understand if… 我完全理解若… / schedule does not allow it 時間不允許 / consider my request 考慮我的請求 / look forward to your reply 期待您的回覆		 I completely understand if your schedule does not allow it. Thank you for considering my request, and I look forward to your reply.
Sign-off 署名
Yours sincerely, 敬上		 Yours sincerely,
Jennifer
