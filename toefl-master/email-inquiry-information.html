Email 04 — Asking About a Photography Workshop 詢問攝影工作坊資訊

Recipient 收件人：Ms. Carter, workshop coordinator 工作坊負責人卡特女士（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a university student who has recently become interested in photography. Last week, you saw a poster for a six-week weekend photography workshop at the City Arts Center, which starts next month. The poster gave only the starting date and a contact email address, so you still have several questions. You have never taken a photography class before, and you do not own a professional camera. Write an email to Ms. Carter, the workshop coordinator. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain why you are interested in the workshop
  • Ask whether the workshop is suitable for beginners and what equipment you need
  • Ask about the schedule and the fee
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + title + family name 職稱＋姓氏		 Dear Ms. Carter,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to ask about 我寫信是想詢問 / photography workshop 攝影工作坊		 I am writing to ask about the weekend photography workshop at the City Arts Center.
Bullet 1 — Reason for interest 感興趣的原因
I am interested in … because 我對…有興趣，因為 / enjoy + V-ing 喜歡… / take photos 拍照 / I would like to learn how to 我想學習如何		 I am interested in this workshop because I enjoy taking photos when I travel, and I would like to learn how to take better pictures.
Bullet 2 — Level + equipment 程度與器材
However 然而 / Could you tell me if…? 可以請您告訴我是否… / suitable for beginners 適合初學者 / I would also like to know whether 我也想知道是否 / bring my own camera 自備相機		 However, I have never taken a photography class before. Could you tell me if the workshop is suitable for beginners? I would also like to know whether I need to bring my own camera.
Bullet 3 — Schedule + fee 時間與費用
In addition 此外 / could you let me know…? 可以請您告知… / what time 幾點 / how much … costs …的費用是多少		 In addition, could you let me know what time the classes start and how much the workshop costs?
Closing 結尾
Thank you for your help 謝謝您的協助 / I look forward to hearing from you 期待您的回覆		 Thank you for your help. I look forward to hearing from you.
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, 謹致問候		 Best regards,
Jennifer

Bullet 1 — Reason for interest 感興趣的原因
have recently developed a keen interest in 最近對…產生濃厚興趣 / particularly 尤其是 / street and travel photography 街頭與旅遊攝影 / be eager to 渴望 / move beyond 超越、不再只停留在 / automatic mode 自動模式		 I have recently developed a keen interest in photography, particularly street and travel photography, and I am eager to move beyond the automatic mode on my phone.
Bullet 2 — Level + equipment 程度與器材
As I have no … 由於我沒有… / formal training 正式訓練 / may I ask whether…? 請問是否… / complete beginners 完全的初學者 / I would also be grateful if you could confirm whether 若您能確認是否…我將十分感激 / participants 學員 / equipment 器材 / borrow 借用		 As I have no formal training, may I ask whether the workshop is suitable for complete beginners? I would also be grateful if you could confirm whether participants must bring their own cameras or whether equipment can be borrowed from the center.
Bullet 3 — Schedule + fee 時間與費用
Could you also let me know…? 也可以請您告知… / session 每堂課 / as + S + V 因為 / part-time job 打工 / Finally, I would appreciate further details about 最後，若能提供…的進一步細節我將不勝感激 / fee 費用 / student discount 學生優惠		 Could you also let me know the exact time of each session, as I have a part-time job on Saturday mornings? Finally, I would appreciate further details about the fee and whether a student discount is available.
