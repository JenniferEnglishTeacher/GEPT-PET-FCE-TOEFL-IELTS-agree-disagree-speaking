Email 03 — Complaining About Noise in the Library 投訴圖書館自習區太吵

Recipient 收件人：Ms. Lin, library manager 圖書館主任林女士（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a university student, and final exams begin next week. For the past two weeks, you have been studying on the third floor of the university library, which is supposed to be a quiet study area. However, groups of students often talk loudly, eat snacks and take phone calls there, especially in the evenings. Yesterday you could not finish reviewing your notes because of the noise, and you finally had to leave. Write an email to Ms. Lin, the library manager. In your email, do the following:
  • Describe the problem in the quiet study area
  • Explain how it has affected your exam preparation
  • Suggest what the library could do to solve the problem
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + title + family name 職稱＋姓氏		 Dear Ms. Lin,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to complain about 我寫信是要投訴 / noise 噪音 / quiet study area 安靜自習區		 I am writing to complain about the noise in the quiet study area of the library.
Bullet 1 — The problem 問題描述
For the past two weeks 過去兩週以來 / have been + V-ing 一直在… / talk loudly 大聲說話 / take phone calls 講電話 / especially 尤其		 For the past two weeks, many students have been talking loudly and taking phone calls there, especially in the evenings.
Bullet 2 — The effect 造成的影響
Because of this 因此 / it is hard for me to 我很難 / concentrate on 專心於 / final exams 期末考 / review my notes 複習筆記		 Because of this, it is very hard for me to concentrate. Final exams start next week, but yesterday I could not finish reviewing my notes.
Bullet 3 — Suggestion 建議
I would suggest + V-ing 我建議 / put up signs 張貼告示 / Quiet Zone 安靜區 / It would also be helpful if… 如果…也會很有幫助 / staff member 館員 / check the area 巡視該區		 I would suggest putting up more "Quiet Zone" signs. It would also be helpful if a staff member could check the area in the evenings.
Closing 結尾
Thank you for your attention 感謝您的關注 / I hope … can be solved soon 希望…能盡快解決		 Thank you for your attention. I hope this problem can be solved soon.

Jennifer

Email 11 — Complaining About Faulty Headphones Ordered Online 投訴網購耳機故障

Recipient 收件人：Customer Service Team of SoundWave 網路商店 SoundWave 客服團隊（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–140 words

Task 題目
You are a university student. Last week, you ordered a pair of wireless headphones from SoundWave, an online electronics store, because you need them for your online classes. The package was delivered yesterday afternoon, and the box looked undamaged. However, when you tested the headphones, the left earpiece produced no sound at all, and there was a crack on the charging case. You have online classes every day, so the problem is very inconvenient. Write an email to the Customer Service Team of SoundWave. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain what you ordered and when it arrived
  • Describe the problem with the product
  • Say what you would like the company to do
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + department / team 部門或團隊名稱（不知道姓名時）		 Dear Customer Service Team,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to complain about 我寫信是要投訴 / wireless headphones 無線耳機 / order … from your website 在貴網站訂購		 I am writing to complain about a pair of wireless headphones that I ordered from your website.
Bullet 1 — The order 訂購內容與到貨時間
place an order 下訂單 / order number 訂單編號 / package 包裹 / be delivered 送達		 I placed the order (No. SW20458) last week, and the package was delivered yesterday afternoon.
Bullet 2 — The problem 商品問題
However, when I… 然而，當我… / I found that 我發現 / faulty 有故障的 / earpiece 耳機單邊 / does not work at all 完全不能用 / crack 裂痕 / charging case 充電盒 / inconvenient 不方便的		 However, when I tested the headphones, I found that they were faulty. The left earpiece does not work at all, and the charging case has a crack. I need them for my online classes, so this is very inconvenient.
Bullet 3 — What you want 希望對方怎麼處理
I would like to ask for 我想要求 / replacement 換貨 / refund 退款 / Could you please tell me how to… 可以請您告訴我如何… / return the item 退回商品		 I would like to ask for a replacement or a refund. Could you please tell me how to return the item?
Closing 結尾
Thank you for your help 謝謝您的協助 / I look forward to your reply 期待您的回覆		 Thank you for your help. I look forward to your reply.
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, 謹致問候		 Best regards,
Jennifer

Email 19 — Raising a Concern About Food Waste in the School Cafeteria 反映學校餐廳剩食問題

Recipient 收件人：Sophie, president of the Student Council 學生會會長 Sophie（semi-formal 半正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–140 words

Task 題目
You are a student at Riverside High School. For the past few weeks, you have noticed that many students leave large amounts of uneaten food on their trays in the school cafeteria every lunchtime. Whole portions of rice, vegetables and fruit are thrown into the bins, while the cafeteria staff keep serving the same large portion to everyone. You think this is a waste of food and money, and that it is also bad for the environment. Write an email to Sophie, the president of the Student Council. In your email, do the following:
  • Describe the problem you have noticed
  • Explain why you think it is a serious problem
  • Suggest what the Student Council could do about it
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + first name 名字（寫給同學／學生會幹部，半正式）		 Dear Sophie,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to tell you about 我寫信是想告訴你 / a problem in… …的一個問題 / school cafeteria 學校餐廳		 I am writing to tell you about a problem in our school cafeteria.
Bullet 1 — The problem 你注意到的問題
Every lunchtime 每天午餐時間 / I see many students + V 我看到很多學生… / uneaten food 沒吃完的食物 / tray 餐盤 / be thrown into the bins 被丟進垃圾桶		 Every lunchtime, I see many students leave uneaten food on their trays. Rice, vegetables and fruit are thrown into the bins.
Bullet 2 — Why it is serious 為什麼嚴重
I think this is a serious problem because 我認為這是個嚴重的問題，因為 / waste 浪費 / It is also bad for 這也對…有害 / the environment 環境		 I think this is a serious problem because it wastes food and money. It is also bad for the environment.
Bullet 3 — Suggestions 建議
I would like to suggest two ideas 我想提出兩個想法 / First… Second… 第一…第二… / put up posters 張貼海報 / remind sb to + V 提醒某人… / take only what they can finish 只拿吃得完的量 / offer smaller portions 提供較小的份量		 I would like to suggest two ideas. First, the Student Council could put up posters to remind students to take only what they can finish. Second, the cafeteria could offer smaller portions for students who are not hungry.
Closing 結尾
I hope you can… 希望你們能… / discuss 討論 / at the next meeting 在下次會議 / Thank you for your time 謝謝你撥出時間		 I hope you can discuss this at the next meeting. Thank you for your time.
Sign-off 署名
Best wishes, 祝好		 Best wishes,
Jennifer
