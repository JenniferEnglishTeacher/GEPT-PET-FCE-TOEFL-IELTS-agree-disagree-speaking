Email 03 — Complaining About Noise in the Library 投訴圖書館自習區太吵

Recipient 收件人：Ms. Lin, library manager 圖書館主任林女士（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a university student, and final exams begin next week. For the past two weeks, you have been studying on the third floor of the university library, which is supposed to be a quiet study area. However, groups of students often talk loudly, eat snacks and take phone calls there, especially in the evenings. Yesterday you could not finish reviewing your notes because of the noise, and you finally had to leave. Write an email to Ms. Lin, the library manager. In your email, do the following:
  • Describe the problem in the quiet study area
  • Explain how it has affected your exam preparation
  • Suggest what the library could do to solve the problem
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Task 題目
Last Saturday evening, you took your family to the Green Garden Restaurant to celebrate your grandmother's 70th birthday. You had booked a table for eight people two weeks earlier. However, when you arrived, the table was not ready, and you had to wait for forty minutes. Later, two of the dishes that came were not the ones you had ordered, and the bill included a dessert that you never received. You were very disappointed. Write an email to Mr. Bradley, the manager of the restaurant. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain when and why you visited the restaurant
  • Describe what went wrong
  • Say what you would like the restaurant to do
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear Mr. + last name 姓氏（寫給餐廳經理，正式）		 Dear Mr. Bradley,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to complain about 我寫信是想投訴 / poor service 很差的服務		 I am writing to complain about the poor service at your restaurant last Saturday.
Bullet 1 — When and why you visited 何時、為何去用餐
celebrate 慶祝 / 70th birthday 七十歲生日 / book a table for + 人數 訂…人的位子 / two weeks earlier 兩週前		 We came to celebrate my grandmother's 70th birthday, and I had booked a table for eight two weeks earlier.
Bullet 2 — What went wrong 出了什麼問題
However 然而 / so we had to + V 所以我們不得不… / In addition 此外 / dish 菜餚 / order 點（餐）/ be charged for 被收取…的費用 / dessert 甜點		 However, when we arrived, our table was not ready, so we had to wait forty minutes. In addition, two of the dishes were not the ones we had ordered, and we were charged for a dessert that we never received.
Bullet 3 — What you want 你希望對方怎麼做
I would be grateful if you could 如果您能…我會很感激 / refund 退還（款項）/ I also hope that 我也希望 / staff 員工 / reservation 訂位 / more carefully 更仔細地		 I would be grateful if you could refund the cost of the dessert. I also hope that your staff will check reservations more carefully.
Closing 結尾
Thank you for your help 謝謝您的協助 / I look forward to your reply 期待您的回覆		 Thank you for your help. I look forward to your reply.
Sign-off 署名
Yours sincerely, 敬上		 Yours sincerely,
Jennifer

I would also suggest that your staff confirm every reservation so that other families do not have the same experience.
