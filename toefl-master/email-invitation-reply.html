Email 05 — Replying to a Birthday Party Invitation 回覆生日派對邀請

Recipient 收件人：Lily, your friend 朋友莉莉（informal 非正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
Your friend Lily has invited you to her birthday party at her home next Saturday evening. You would love to go, but your parents are planning a family gathering on the same weekend, and they have not decided on the exact day and time yet. You do not want to promise Lily that you will come and then cancel at the last minute, so you cannot give her a definite answer right now. Write an email to Lily. In your email, do the following:
  • Thank her for the invitation
  • Explain why you are not sure whether you can attend
  • Tell her when you can give her a final answer
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Email 45 — Inviting a Graduate to Speak at Career Day 邀請校友擔任職涯日講者

Recipient 收件人：Dr. Hsu, a veterinarian who graduated from your school 校友許醫師（獸醫）（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a member of the student council at Minghua High School, which is organizing its annual Career Day next month. The event gives students a chance to learn about different jobs before they choose a university major. The council would like to invite graduates to talk about their jobs. One of them is Dr. Hsu, a veterinarian who graduated from your school fifteen years ago and now runs an animal hospital in the city. Many students are interested in working with animals, so you have been asked to contact her. Write an email to Dr. Hsu. In your email, do the following:
  • Invite her to speak at Career Day and explain why you chose her
  • Give the details of the event (for example, date, time, place and length of the talk)
  • Ask her to reply by a certain date and to tell you what she will need
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.
