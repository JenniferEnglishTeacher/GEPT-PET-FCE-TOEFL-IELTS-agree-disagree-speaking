Email 05 — Replying to a Birthday Party Invitation 回覆生日派對邀請

Recipient 收件人：Lily, your friend 朋友莉莉（informal 非正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
Your friend Lily has invited you to her birthday party at her home next Saturday evening. You would love to go, but your parents are planning a family gathering on the same weekend, and they have not decided on the exact day and time yet. You do not want to promise Lily that you will come and then cancel at the last minute, so you cannot give her a definite answer right now. Write an email to Lily. In your email, do the following:
  • Thank her for the invitation
  • Explain why you are not sure whether you can attend
  • Tell her when you can give her a final answer
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Hi + first name 名字（informal 非正式）		 Hi Lily,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I hope you are doing well 希望你一切都好 / I am writing to reply to 我寫信是想回覆 / invitation 邀請		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to reply to your invitation.
Bullet 1 — Thank her 感謝邀請
Thank you so much for + V-ing 非常謝謝你… / invite 邀請 / birthday party 生日派對 / It sounds like 聽起來像是 / celebration 慶祝活動		 Thank you so much for inviting me to your birthday party next Saturday. It sounds like a fun and special celebration.
Bullet 2 — Explain the situation 說明情況
However, I am not sure if I can 然而我不確定是否能 / attend 出席 / family gathering 家庭聚會 / confirm 確認		 However, I am not sure if I can attend. My parents are planning a family gathering that weekend, but they have not confirmed the time yet.
Bullet 3 — When you can answer 何時答覆
I should know by + 時間 我應該在…之前會知道 / whether 是否 / be free 有空 / I will let you know as soon as 我一…就會告訴你 / final answer 最後答覆		 I should know by Friday whether I am free in the evening, and I will let you know as soon as I have a final answer.
Closing 結尾
I really hope I can 我真的希望我能 / celebrate together 一起慶祝 / Thank you again for 再次感謝你 / think of 想到		 I really hope I can join you and celebrate together. Thank you again for thinking of me.
Sign-off 署名
Best wishes, 祝好		 Best wishes,
Jennifer

Email 13 — Inviting a Classmate to a Farewell Gathering 邀請同學參加歡送會

Recipient 收件人：Ryan, your close classmate 好同學 Ryan（informal 非正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
Your classmate Mia, who has been in your study group for three years, is transferring to a school in Australia next month. You are planning a small surprise farewell gathering for her at your home on the last Saturday before she leaves, and you want your close classmate Ryan to come. You would also like your friends to prepare something special so that Mia has good memories to take with her. Because you need to order food, you have to know soon how many people are coming. Write an email to Ryan. In your email, do the following:
  • Invite him and explain why you are organizing the gathering
  • Give the details of the gathering (date, time and place)
  • Ask him to help with something and to reply by a certain day
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Hi + first name 名字（informal 非正式）		 Hi Ryan,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I hope you are doing well 希望你一切都好 / I am writing to invite you to 我寫信是想邀請你參加 / farewell gathering 歡送會		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to invite you to a small farewell gathering for Mia.
Bullet 1 — Invite + reason 邀請與原因
As you probably know 你大概已經知道 / transfer to 轉學到 / so I would like to 所以我想要 / proper 像樣的 / send-off 送別		 As you probably know, she is transferring to a school in Australia next month, so I would like to give her a proper send-off.
Bullet 2 — Details 活動細節
will take place at + 地點 + on + 日期 將在…舉行 / from … to … 從…到… / surprise 驚喜 / so please do not 所以請不要		 The gathering will take place at my home on Saturday, June 20, from 4 to 7 p.m. It is a surprise, so please do not tell her.
Bullet 3 — Ask for help + reply 請求協助與回覆
Could you please + V 可以請你…嗎 / bring 帶 / memory book 回憶紀念冊 / Please let me know by + 時間 + if you can make it 請在…之前告訴我你能否參加 / so that 以便 / order 訂購		 Could you please bring a photo of you and Mia for a memory book? Please let me know by Wednesday if you can make it so that I can order food.
Closing 結尾
I really hope you can 我真的希望你能 / come 來		 I really hope you can come.
Sign-off 署名
See you soon, 再見囉		 See you soon,
Jennifer
