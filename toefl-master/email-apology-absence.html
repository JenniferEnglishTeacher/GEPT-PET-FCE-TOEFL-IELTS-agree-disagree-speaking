Email 01 — Missing a Club Meeting 無法出席社團會議

Recipient 收件人：Alex, club president 社長（semi-formal 半正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a member of your university's Environmental Club. The club will hold its monthly planning meeting tomorrow afternoon to discuss a campus recycling campaign, and you promised to present your poster design at the meeting. However, you have a medical appointment at the same time, and it cannot be changed. Write an email to Alex, the club president. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain why you cannot attend the meeting
  • Apologize for your absence
  • Ask what you should prepare for the next meeting
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Hi + name		 Hi Alex,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
hope … doing well / let you know 告知 / cannot come 無法出席		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to let you know that I cannot come to tomorrow's club meeting.
Bullet 1 — Reason 原因
Unfortunately 可惜 / medical appointment 看診預約 / cannot be changed 無法更改 / only free this week 只有這週有空		 Unfortunately, I have a medical appointment after class, and it cannot be changed because the doctor is only free this week.
Bullet 2 — Apology 道歉
sorry for + V-ing 為…抱歉 / especially because 尤其因為 / promised 答應過 / poster design 海報設計 / recycling campaign 回收活動		 I am very sorry for missing the meeting, especially because I promised to show my poster design for the recycling campaign.
Bullet 3 — Request 請求
Could you please tell me…? 可以請你告訴我…嗎 / prepare 準備 / I would be happy to 樂意 / tasks 任務 / send by email tonight 今晚寄出		 Could you please tell me what I should prepare for the next meeting? I would be happy to finish any tasks before then, and I can send you my poster by email tonight.
Closing 結尾
thank 感謝 / understanding 體諒		 Thank you for your understanding.
Sign-off 署名
Best wishes, 祝好		 Best wishes,
Jennifer

Email 09 — Missing a Class Because of Illness 因病缺課向教授說明

Recipient 收件人：Professor Davis 戴維斯教授（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are taking Professor Davis's Introduction to Business course at your university. Yesterday you missed the class because you had a high fever and had to see a doctor. A classmate told you that during that class, the professor explained the requirements for an important group presentation and assigned some homework. You have never missed this class before. You are worried about falling behind, and you want to show that you take the course seriously. Write an email to Professor Davis. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain why you were absent and apologize
  • Ask about the homework and the material you missed
  • Ask for advice on how to catch up
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + title + family name 稱謂＋姓氏		 Dear Professor Davis,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
hope … doing well / explain why 說明原因 / missed your class 缺課		 I hope you are doing well. I am writing to explain why I missed your Introduction to Business class yesterday.
Bullet 1 — Reason + apology 原因與道歉
Unfortunately 可惜 / high fever 發高燒 / see a doctor 看醫生 / rest at home 在家休息 / sorry for + V-ing 為…抱歉		 Unfortunately, I had a high fever, so I had to see a doctor and rest at home. I am very sorry for missing the class.
Bullet 2 — Homework 詢問作業
Could you please tell me…? 可以請您告訴我…嗎 / homework 作業 / I would also like to know 我也想知道 / chapter 章節 / cover 講授 / slides 投影片		 Could you please tell me what homework was given? I would also like to know which chapter you covered and whether the slides are online.
Bullet 3 — Advice 請求建議
In addition 此外 / advice on how to 關於如何…的建議 / catch up 趕上進度 / worried about 擔心 / group presentation 小組報告 / fall behind 落後		 In addition, do you have any advice on how to catch up? I am worried about the group presentation because I do not want to fall behind.
Closing 結尾
thank 感謝 / time 時間 / understanding 體諒		 Thank you for your time and understanding.
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, 敬祝安好		 Best regards,
Jennifer
