Email 07 — Applying for a Part-time Job at a Café 應徵咖啡廳兼職工作

Recipient 收件人：Ms. Lee, the café manager 咖啡廳經理李女士（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a university student, and you would like to earn some money and gain work experience. Yesterday you saw a notice on the door of Sunrise Café, a popular coffee shop near your campus, saying that it is looking for a part-time assistant to take orders and serve customers on weekday evenings and weekends. You have never worked in a café before, but you enjoy talking to people and you are a fast learner. Write an email to Ms. Lee, the café manager. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain why you are interested in the job
  • Tell her when you are available to work
  • Ask about the next step in the application process
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.
