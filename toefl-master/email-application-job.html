Email 07 — Applying for a Part-time Job at a Café 應徵咖啡廳兼職工作

Recipient 收件人：Ms. Lee, the café manager 咖啡廳經理李女士（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a university student, and you would like to earn some money and gain work experience. Yesterday you saw a notice on the door of Sunrise Café, a popular coffee shop near your campus, saying that it is looking for a part-time assistant to take orders and serve customers on weekday evenings and weekends. You have never worked in a café before, but you enjoy talking to people and you are a fast learner. Write an email to Ms. Lee, the café manager. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain why you are interested in the job
  • Tell her when you are available to work
  • Ask about the next step in the application process
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Sample 1 — Level 1 基礎版（Score 4 · CEFR B1–B2 · about 105 words）

Element + key words 架構與提示字Your writing 學生書寫區Sample 範文
Greeting 稱呼
Dear + Ms. / Mr. + last name 姓氏（formal 正式）		 Dear Ms. Lee,
Opening + purpose 開頭與目的
I am writing to apply for 我寫信是想應徵 / part-time 兼職的 / assistant 助理 / position 職位		 I am writing to apply for the part-time assistant position at Sunrise Café.
Bullet 1 — Why you are interested 感興趣的原因
I am interested in … because 我對…有興趣，因為 / enjoy + V-ing 喜歡 / I believe that … would help me + V 我相信…能幫助我 / communication skills 溝通能力 / responsible 負責任的		 I am interested in this job because I enjoy talking to people and learning new skills. I believe that working in a café would help me improve my communication skills and become more responsible.
Bullet 2 — Your availability 可上班時間
I am available on 我…有空 / weekday evenings 平日晚上 / all day on weekends 週末整天 / I can start + V-ing 我可以開始		 I am available on weekday evenings after 5 p.m. and all day on weekends. I can start working next week.
Bullet 3 — Ask about the next step 詢問下一步
Could you please tell me 可以請您告訴我 / the next step 下一步 / I would also like to know whether 我也想知道是否 / prepare documents 準備文件 / interview 面試		 Could you please tell me what the next step is? I would also like to know whether I need to prepare any documents for an interview.
Closing 結尾
Thank you for your consideration 感謝您的考慮 / I look forward to hearing from you 期待您的回覆		 Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.
Sign-off 署名
Best regards, 誠摯的問候		 Best regards,
Jennifer

Email 15 — Applying for a Volunteer Position at an Animal Shelter 應徵動物收容所志工

Recipient 收件人：Mr. Harris, the volunteer coordinator 志工協調員哈里斯先生（formal 正式）｜ Time 時間：7 minutes ｜ Target 目標字數：100–130 words

Task 題目
You are a university student who loves animals. Last week you read on the website of Happy Paws Animal Shelter, which is a short bus ride from your campus, that it needs weekend volunteers to feed the dogs and cats, clean their living areas and take the dogs for walks. You have looked after your family's dog for six years, and you once helped at a pet adoption event at your school. Write an email to Mr. Harris, the volunteer coordinator. In your email, do the following:
  • Explain why you would like to volunteer at the shelter
  • Describe your experience with animals
  • Ask what training or preparation volunteers need
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Bullet 1 — Why you want to volunteer 想當志工的原因
I would like to … because 我想要…，因為 / hope to + V 希望 / meaningful 有意義的 / in my free time 在空閒時間		 I would like to volunteer because I love animals and hope to do something meaningful in my free time.
Bullet 2 — Your experience 相關經驗
take care of 照顧 / so I know how to + V 所以我知道如何 / feed 餵食 / walk dogs 遛狗 / pet adoption event 寵物認養活動		 I have taken care of my family's dog for six years, so I know how to feed and walk dogs. Last year, I also helped at a pet adoption event at my school.

Closing 結尾

Task 題目
You are a university student who enjoys working with children. On your university's job board, you saw that Sunshine English Summer Camp is hiring camp counselors to lead games, songs and simple English activities for children aged seven to twelve in July and August. You tutored your younger cousin in English for a year, and you were the leader of your high school's guitar club. Your final exams end on June 28. Write an email to Ms. Parker, the camp director. In your email, do the following:
  • Describe the skills and experience that make you suitable for the job
  • Say when you are available to work this summer
  • Ask about the interview and the documents you need to send
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.
